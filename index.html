SimForge Uploader

SimForge Uploader is an internal tool used only by SimForge Studio. It connects to the YouTube Data API so the studio can upload the videos it produces to its own channels listed above and read basic performance statistics for those channels. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.

Read how the tool handles data in our Privacy Policy and the conditions of use in our Terms of Service.
